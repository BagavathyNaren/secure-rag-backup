Finance and Expense Policy - TechCorp Inc.

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Section 1: Expense Approval
Expenses under $500 require direct manager approval.
Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require department head approval.
Expenses over $5,000 require VP-level approval and Finance review.

Section 2: Travel Policy
Domestic flights must be booked economy class. Business class is permitted
for flights over 6 hours with VP approval. Hotel rates are capped at $250 per night in standard markets.
Hotel rates are capped at $350 per night in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, London).
Per diem for meals is $75 per day domestic.
Per diem for meals is $100 per day international.

Section 3: Reimbursement
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of approval.
Late submissions (beyond 30 days) require VP exception approval.

Section 4: Corporate Cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees at manager level and above.
Monthly statements must be reconciled within 5 business days.
Personal use of corporate cards is strictly prohibited and grounds for termination.

Section 5: Budget Planning
Annual budgets are submitted by department heads in Q4 for the following year.
Mid-year budget adjustments require CFO approval.
Departments exceeding budget by more than 10% must submit a corrective action plan.
