Information Security Policy - TechCorp Inc.

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Section 1: Data Classification
All company data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Confidential.
Confidential data includes customer PII, financial records, and trade secrets.
Internal data includes project documentation, internal communications, and org charts.

Section 2: Access Control
Access to Confidential data requires manager approval and completion of security
training. Role-based access control (RBAC) is enforced across all systems.
Access reviews are conducted quarterly by the Security team.

Section 3: Incident Response
All security incidents must be reported to security@techcorp.com within 1 hour
of discovery. The incident response team will triage within 4 hours.
Critical incidents trigger automatic notification to the CTO and legal counsel.
A post-mortem report is required within 5 business days of resolution.

Section 4: Password Policy
Passwords must be minimum 14 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and
special characters. Password rotation is required every 90 days.
Password reuse of the last 12 passwords is prohibited.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems.

Section 5: Vendor Security
All third-party vendors must complete a security questionnaire before onboarding.
Vendors handling Confidential data must provide SOC 2 Type II certification.
Annual vendor security reviews are mandatory.

Section 6: Network Security
Use of public Wi-Fi is prohibited without VPN. All remote connections must use the company-approved VPN.
